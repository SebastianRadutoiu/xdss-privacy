SandTide privacy policy

Publisher: XDSS · Contact: contact@xdss.net · Effective date: 18 November 2026

SandTide is a sand-building game for Android (package com.xdss.sandtide): dig, pile and shape sand on a beach and watch the tide come in. This page explains what the app stores, what the services built into it collect, and the choices you have. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours: the game keeps everything on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your beaches: the current beach (saved automatically) and up to five saved beaches, each with a small preview picture.
  • Your collection of finds, beach goals completed, and the starfish you earned.
  • Photos you take in photo mode, kept in the game's own gallery. When you capture a photo, a copy is also saved to your phone's Pictures/SandTide folder; it stays there if you delete it in the game.
  • Settings: graphics quality, frame rate, tide and day length, tool dock side, sound, music, vibration, how fast sand dries, and similar options.
  • Whether you own Premium (as a cache; Google Play is checked again on start), and your answer to the consent form, so the app does not ask again on every start.

This data stays on your device. Android may include it in your device backup if you have that turned on. We do not receive any of it. A photo leaves your device only if you choose Share and pick an app to send it with.

Services in the app and what they collect

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Players without Premium see a banner on the title screen and its menus, and can choose to watch an ad to try one Premium item (such as a bucket shape or a photo look) for the rest of the session. There are no ads while you build or in photo mode, and no full-screen ads you did not ask for. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device and usage data such as the advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device model, interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Depending on your consent, ads can be personalised or non-personalised. How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested. Your choice is stored on your device.

Google Play Billing. Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you own Premium; it never sees your payment details. Google Payments privacy notice.

The app has no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and the game engine's (Unity's) online services are turned off.

Your choices

  • Privacy options: Settings > Privacy options reopens the consent form, where you can change or withdraw consent (shown where the law requires it).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most devices).
  • No ads: Premium removes all ads; with Premium, no ads are loaded.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check your Premium purchase with Google Play. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the ads SDK as described above.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Premium.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback while building; turn it off in Settings > Vibration.

Saving photos to Pictures uses Android's media store for the app's own images and needs no storage permission. The app does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or access to your other photos and files.

Children

SandTide is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it, except photo copies in your Pictures folder, which you can delete in your gallery. Data collected by Google's services is kept and deleted according to Google's policies linked above; you can also reset your advertising ID and withdraw consent at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date; important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
