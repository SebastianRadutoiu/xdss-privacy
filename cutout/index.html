Cutlight privacy policy

Publisher: XDSS · Effective date: 17 November 2026

Cutlight is a photo editor. It cuts out subjects, changes backgrounds, blurs backgrounds by depth and erases objects with AI models that run on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded. This page explains what the app stores, what the Google services inside it collect, and the choices you have.

Your photos and edits

  • When you open a photo, Cutlight copies it into the app's private storage on your phone, together with your edits (the list of changes, the masks and depth maps the models make, brush strokes, background photos you pick and a small thumbnail).
  • All of this stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync and no Cutlight server. The AI models are part of the app and run offline.
  • Exported images are saved to your gallery (Pictures/Cutlight) or handed to the app you choose when you share. Exports contain no location or camera metadata.
  • Project files are not included in Android backups.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a box ad on the export, settings and licences screens, an occasional full-screen ad after you save, and an optional ad you can watch to try the object eraser for 24 hours. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the advertising ID (not for users who may be under 16, see below), device information (such as model, OS version and settings), app interactions with ads, and an approximate location derived from the IP address. With your consent ads may be personalised. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), Cutlight asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads or analytics use your data. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google or privacy settings.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Cutlight sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens and tools are used, whether an AI step succeeded and roughly how long it took (in coarse buckets), exports by format, and ad and purchase events, with device information and an app instance identifier. It never sends your photos, anything you type, file names, colours or positions you choose, or anything you create. Statistics follow your consent choice, and you can turn them off in Settings → Usage statistics.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Cutlight receives only the purchase status (to unlock Premium); it never sees your payment details. See the Google Payments privacy notice. Premium removes all ads.

Reporting a problem

"Report a problem" opens your email app with a pre-filled message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and it contains only what you choose to write or attach.

Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: ads, the consent form, usage statistics and purchases. Editing works offline.
  • AD_ID and the Android ad services permissions: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the advertising ID and the privacy-preserving ad APIs.
  • BILLING: Google Play purchases.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: only on Android 8 and 9, to save exports to the gallery.
  • Some Google libraries add WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE and the install-referrer permission for their own background work.

Cutlight does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or read access to your photo library: photos are chosen with the system Photo Picker or taken with your camera app.

Your year of birth, teens and children

Cutlight is made for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The first time you open the app it asks for your year of birth. Only the year is stored, on your phone (it is not sent anywhere and is not included in backups), and it is used only to choose how ads and usage statistics work:

  • If you may be under 16, Cutlight treats you as a child: ads are requested with Google's child protections (no advertising ID, no personalised ads or remarketing, only ads rated for general audiences), and no usage statistics are sent.
  • Otherwise ads and usage statistics work as described above.

If you believe a child under 13 has used Cutlight and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting data

Your projects stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it, which removes them. Data collected by Google's services is kept according to Google's policies linked above; you can reset your advertising ID and change your consent at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date. Material changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes.
