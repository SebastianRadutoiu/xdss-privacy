Jamaway privacy policy

Publisher: XDSS · Contact: contact@xdss.net · Effective date: 14 November 2026

Jamaway is a traffic puzzle game for Android (package com.xdss.traffic). This page explains what the app stores, what the services built into it collect, and the choices you have. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours: the game keeps your progress on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: which levels you cleared and their stars, daily challenge results, statistics (levels cleared, streaks, hints used, undos).
  • Games in progress (the board, its history and the time played), so you can continue where you left off.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, reduce motion, art theme, the hints you have left today and hints earned from ads, and which tutorials and tips you have seen.
  • Your Premium purchase state and your answer to the consent form, so the app does not ask again on every start.

This data stays on your device. Progress and settings can be included in Android's own device backup (Google's backup service, if you have it turned on); the purchase state and the consent answer are excluded from backup. We do not receive any of it.

Services in the app and what they collect

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Free players see ads: a banner on the home screen, a 300x250 ad on the result and statistics screens, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for an extra hint. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects device and usage data such as the advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device model, app interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Depending on your consent, ads can be personalised or non-personalised. How Google uses data from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads or analytics are used. Your choice is stored on your device.

Google Analytics for Firebase. If you allow it in the consent form and keep "Usage statistics" on in Settings, the app sends anonymous usage events: which screens are opened, level started and finished (level number or mode, duration, bumps, hints), hints used, ads shown, and purchases. These are predefined values only; nothing you type or create is ever sent. Firebase also uses an app instance identifier and device information. Firebase privacy information.

Google Play Billing. Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether you own Premium; it never sees your payment details. Google Payments privacy notice.

Your choices

  • Privacy choices: Settings > Privacy choices reopens the consent form, where you can change or withdraw consent (shown where the law requires it).
  • Usage statistics: Settings > Usage statistics turns analytics off at any time and resets the analytics data on your device.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most devices).
  • No ads: Premium removes all ads; with Premium, the ads SDK is not started at all.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, check your Premium purchase with Google Play, and send usage statistics if you allowed them. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the ads SDK as described above.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Premium.
  • Vibration: for the short haptic feedback when you tap, bump or clear a level (turn it off in Settings > Haptics).

The app does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Jamaway is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it. Data collected by Google's services is kept and deleted according to Google's policies linked above; you can also reset your advertising ID and withdraw consent at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date; important changes are also mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
